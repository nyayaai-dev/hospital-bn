Forecast log

All patients, capacities and surveillance readings on this page are simulated for demonstration. The model is an hour-by-hour flow simulation: arrivals by hour and weekday, seasonal illness curves for India, routing to wards, length of stay, discharge timing, lab and imaging queues, and nurse ratios. The shaded band is the range between lower (−10% arrivals) and higher (+10%) demand runs. Forecasts support decisions by hospital staff; they do not make them.

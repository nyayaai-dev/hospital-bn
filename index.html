BMC disease reports

When BMC releases monsoon illness figures (a press note, ward report or IDSP weekly report), enter the period and the counts. A report that ended in the last 21 days and covers up to 5 weeks rescales each disease's current rate to match it. Longer or older reports are kept as a record. Reports are shared by every hospital on this device.

        Import census data

        Paste, upload or drag in a CSV exported from your HIS or Excel, one row per hour or per day. Only a date column is required. Commas, semicolons and tabs all work, and common headings are recognised (for example Date, Casualty attendance, ICU census, IPD admissions). Dates can be 14/10/2026 08:00, 2026-10-14 08:00, 14-Oct-2026 or a date column plus an hour column. Days come before months. The last row becomes the forecast's starting point, ED arrivals calibrate demand, and earlier rows feed the accuracy check.

        timestamp, ed_arrivals, ed_census, ed_waiting_for_bed, med_occupied, surg_occupied, icu_occupied, peds_occupied, iso_occupied, lab_backlog, platelet_stock, oxygen_stock, ambulances_busy, admissions, discharges

        With 7+ days of rows the app learns casualty attendances and admissions per day, average stay (occupied beds ÷ admissions) and occupancy. Hourly rows also teach the hour-of-day arrival pattern, and 14+ days the weekday arrival and discharge patterns. Learned values replace placeholders and are marked I.

        Data source notes and hospital names stay in English. Each alert email also keeps an English version for official records.

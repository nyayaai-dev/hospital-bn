        Import census data

        Paste or upload a CSV exported from your HIS, one row per hour or per day. Only timestamp is required. The last row becomes the forecast's starting point, ED arrivals calibrate demand, and earlier rows feed the accuracy check.

        timestamp, ed_arrivals, ed_census, ed_waiting_for_bed, med_occupied, surg_occupied, icu_occupied, peds_occupied, iso_occupied, lab_backlog, platelet_stock, oxygen_stock, ambulances_busy
